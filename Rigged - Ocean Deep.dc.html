About Rigged

Everything offshore

RIGGED was created by offshore workers who understand the industry and the challenges that come with it. Across oil & gas, renewables and marine, we know how difficult it can be to find the right jobs, keep track of certifications and get genuine insight into the platforms and vessels we work on.

RIGGED is built to bring the offshore industry together as one connected community, giving workers, recruiters and training providers a place to connect, share knowledge and discover new opportunities.
