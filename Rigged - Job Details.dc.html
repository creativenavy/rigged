About the role

We are recruiting an experienced derrickman for a semi-submersible operating in the Central North Sea, on a three-week-on, three-week-off rotation. Helicopter transfer from Aberdeen heliport. Accommodation, meals, and PPE provided on board.

Day to day you will handle pipe in the derrick, maintain the racking system, and stand in as assistant driller during shift changes. You will report to the driller and work as part of a permanent team.

What we expect

  • Minimum three years offshore, at least one year in a derrick position
  • Valid BOSIET, HUET, and offshore medical certificates
  • Familiarity with hydraulic racking systems
  • Right to work in the United Kingdom
  • A calm, methodical attitude and a strong safety mindset — this team has run three years without a lost-time incident
